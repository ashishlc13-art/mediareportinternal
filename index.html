1. Executive Summary

Following the strategic partnership with the Ujwal Thapa Foundation, our team led the branding, marketing, and distribution of the 1-hour 50-minute documentary Dimag Ghochne Manche. This project successfully launched D Go’s new visual identity, positioning us as a socially responsible leader in the Nepali OTT market.

We achieved massive visibility across 16 media outlets with Zero Marketing Spend, saving the company capital while building significant brand equity.

2. Strategic Branding Pillars

Visual Identity

New logo integration across all high-quality social assets and platform UI.

Viral Engagement

Distributed 5 targeted video segments focusing on "Reality over Pranks."

Earned PR

Strategic nationwide press release securing top-tier organic news placements.
